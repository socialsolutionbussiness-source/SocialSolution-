How We Work

A simple process, from topic to published post

Our content management service is built around one idea: you record, and we manage the rest.

You provide the raw footage. You record videos on the topics we agree on and upload them to Google Drive. Social Solution handles the planning, research, editing, creative direction, graphics where needed, and the uploading or scheduling.

  1. 1
    Together

    Step 1: Plan the topics

    We agree on topics together. We handle the research and content planning, so you know what to record.

  2. 2
    You

    Step 2: Record your videos

    You record on the agreed topics, in your own voice and at your own pace.

  3. 3
    You

    Step 3: Upload the raw footage

    You upload the raw footage to Google Drive, and we pick it up from there.

  4. 4
    Social Solution

    Step 4: Edit and create

    We edit your videos and guide the creative direction, with graphics added where needed.

  5. 5
    Together

    Step 5: Review

    You review the finished content and share your feedback before it goes live.

  6. 6
    Social Solution

    Step 6: Publish

    With your permission, we upload or schedule the content on your social media accounts.
